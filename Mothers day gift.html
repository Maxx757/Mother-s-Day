Mother's Day, May 2025
Dear Mum,

Words have never quite been able to hold the weight of what I want to say to you. But today, I want to try — to trace the shape of everything you have given me, and everything we have grown through together.

You have been there for every stumble and every triumph. You have laughed with me in the best moments and held space for me in the hardest ones. And somewhere in the middle of all of it, you became not just my mother, but one of my greatest inspirations.

This page is a small piece of my heart — a collection of our shared moments, the memories I carry like warm light, and the story of how I have grown because of you. Thank you for every second, minute, hour, days, weeks, months and years. Yours truely Maxwell Nduati Mwangi (Duati).

With endless love, Maxwell 🌸
